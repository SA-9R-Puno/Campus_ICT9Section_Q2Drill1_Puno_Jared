Pickleball Guide

Learn the basics of one of the fastest-growing sports!

What is Pickleball?

Pickleball is a fun sport that combines elements of tennis, badminton, and table tennis. It is played using paddles, a lightweight plastic ball, and a net. People of different ages and skill levels can enjoy this exciting game.

Pickleball Basics

Equipment

You need a pickleball paddle, a plastic ball with holes, a net, and comfortable sports shoes.

Basic Rules

Serve diagonally, let the ball bounce once on each side after the serve, and avoid volleying inside the non-volley zone.

Health Benefits

Pickleball helps improve coordination, balance, cardiovascular fitness, and social interaction.
